# Tidy - product notes for the launch video

Tidy is a web app that cleans up messy CSV files: it detects broken dates, duplicate rows and mixed units, shows a diff, and exports a clean file.
Audience: data analysts who lose an hour a week to spreadsheet cleanup.
Call to action: try it free at tidy.example.
Launch: the video goes in the GitHub README and on X (Twitter). Narrated with a voice-over.
Demo account: the app runs at http://localhost:3000 with seeded sample data (sales.csv, 2,000 rows).
Features to mention: auto-detect issues, one-click fix, side-by-side diff, export, undo history, team sharing, API.
